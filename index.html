FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Something we didn't cover? Ask us directly.

What exactly do I get for $99/month?
A custom-designed website (no templates), SEO setup, hosting, security, ongoing maintenance, access to your manager portal, and unlimited change requests handled by a real person. One flat rate with no setup fees and no surprise invoices.
What does "unlimited change requests" actually mean?
Exactly what it sounds like. New menu, updated hours, swapped photos, a new page, different copy: send it through your portal and we handle it, most requests same-day. No monthly cap, no per-change fee.
How does the +$50/month per domain work?
Your first website is $99/month. Each additional site (a second location, a second brand, another business) is $50/month more, with the same features, its own domain, and its own branding, all managed from one portal.
How long until my site is live?
Typically days, not months. After our first conversation we build a preview for you to review. Once you approve it, we point your domain and you're live, usually within the same week for standard sites.
What about custom projects? What do those cost?
It depends on the project, which is why we quote each one individually. Tell us your idea, we'll scope it together, and you'll get a clear, fixed quote before any work starts.
What happens if I cancel?
You can cancel anytime; there's no long-term contract. We'll work with you on an off-boarding plan, including transferring your domain if you own it.
