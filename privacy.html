privacy policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

EC Web Co ("EC Web Co," "we," "us," or "our") builds and manages websites for small businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit ecwebco.com or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and run our business:

  • Information you give us. When you fill out our contact form or email us, we collect your name, business name, email address, and anything you include in your message.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and any analytics tools may record basic technical data such as your browser type, device, approximate location, and the pages you view. This helps us keep the site secure and understand what's useful.

how we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide a quote or proposal;
  • Deliver, maintain, and improve our services and website;
  • Communicate with you about a project or your account; and
  • Meet our legal, security, and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we don't send marketing emails you didn't ask for.

how your information is stored and shared

Contact-form submissions and project information are stored securely with trusted third-party infrastructure providers we use to host our website and database. We share information only when necessary to operate our business, and only with:

  • Service providers (for example, hosting, database, and email providers) that process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations;
  • Authorities or others when required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

cookies

Our site uses minimal cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality and, where enabled, basic analytics. You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal or record-keeping requirements. When it's no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or opt out of communications, at any time. Just email evan@ecwebco.com and we'll take care of it.

third-party links

Our website and portfolio may link to sites we don't operate. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, so please review their policies separately.

children's privacy

Our website is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be reflected on this page.

contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at evan@ecwebco.com.
